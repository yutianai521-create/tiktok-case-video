# Audio-led cutdowns

Use this for AMAs, X Spaces, podcasts, interviews, or livestreams where audio is the strongest source.

## Find the exact quote

1. Search the clean transcript for the chosen tension.
2. Extract a wider audio window around the approximate timestamp.
3. Transcribe that smaller window with word timestamps and listen to it.
4. Set final boundaries at breaths or complete phrases.

Transcript timestamps are search hints. They can drift and must not be the only basis for the cut.

## Default 10–15 second structure

| Time | Job | Typical treatment |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | Hook and attribution | Short narration or kinetic text naming who asked whom |
| 3–12s | Original quote | Speaker portrait, name, waveform, synchronized phrases |
| 12–15s | Landing | One implication or audience question |

Shorten the hook when the quote needs more time. Do not unnaturally speed up source speech.

## Dialogue integrity

- If two lines are separated in the source, cut to a new shot and label the sequence `EDITED EXCERPT`.
- Do not make a delayed response appear immediate when the delay changes the meaning.
- Do not imply that a connection failure was caused by a preceding question without evidence.
- Show a later company explanation as a separate statement, not as proof contained in the live audio.
- Preserve enough setup to make a provocative quote accurate. A line such as “rules are just power” needs the speaker’s own limiting principle if omitting it would reverse the meaning.

## Visual system without live video

- Speaker portrait or profile card.
- Name and relevant role.
- Waveform driven by the real audio amplitude.
- One or two lines of synchronized text.
- Email, post, or public-log evidence after the speaker is established.
- A small source label such as `PIP’S ANSWER / ORIGINAL AMA AUDIO`.

## Narration handoff

Provide separate clips or clearly timed lines:

```text
00:00–00:03.00
Henry asked Pip about upgrading its AI model.

00:03.00–00:13.90
No voiceover. Original Pip audio is included.

00:13.90–00:15.00
No voiceover. Hold the closing question on screen.
```
